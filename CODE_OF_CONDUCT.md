# Code of conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and professional community.
Examples of positive behavior include showing empathy, accepting constructive
feedback, focusing on technical merit, and taking responsibility for mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal
attacks, unwanted sexual attention, publishing another person's private
information, and sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, community
events, and when representing the project elsewhere. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation
for behavior they reasonably consider inappropriate.

Report conduct incidents privately to the repository maintainers
([@cocofhu](https://github.com/cocofhu)). Do not report conduct incidents in a
public issue.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Retaliation against a person
who makes a good-faith report is prohibited.
